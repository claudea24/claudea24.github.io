Montaj

A beat-locked, AI-assisted Instagram reel editor. You upload travel photos and clips, and an AI director (Claude) scores each shot and arranges them into a story arc of hook, build, climax, and outro. The app detects the soundtrack's tempo in the browser and snaps cuts to the beat. You can refine the result on a CapCut-style timeline with a live 9:16 preview, transitions, and captions.

Built with: Next.js, TypeScript, Remotion, Claude, Supabase, Clerk

AI Photo Editor

Edit photos with plain-English instructions. A LangGraph agent finds the subject with GPT-4o, builds a pixel-precise mask, and inpaints with DALL·E 2. It then scores each result on edit quality, preservation, and realism, and retries with a refined prompt until the edit meets your satisfaction threshold.

Built with: Python, LangGraph, GPT-4o, DALL·E 2, Streamlit

Boston Transit

A real-time MBTA dashboard that shows every bus, subway, commuter-rail, and ferry vehicle moving on a full-screen map of Boston. Tap any stop for live arrivals, or plan trips that are drawn along real tracks and streets. Routes are ranked by the current weather. A background worker streams GTFS-RT feeds into Supabase Realtime.

Built with: Next.js, MapLibre, Supabase Realtime, Railway, Clerk
